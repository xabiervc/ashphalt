# Cinematics and presentation specification

## Presentation language

Use short in-engine scenes, radio, environmental storytelling, rival introductions, event staging, and aftermath shots. Avoid long cinematics that repeatedly remove control.

## Required moments

- Starting Line invitation.
- First Fire Key clue.
- First Authority escalation.
- First rival defeat or rescue.
- City entering a new state.
- Workshop recovery after major damage.
- The Gate reveal.
- Final Fire Key decision.

## Camera rules

Gameplay camera remains player-controlled except for short, skippable transitions. Cinematic cameras use safe framing, subtitle support, audio description hooks, and skip/replay options.
