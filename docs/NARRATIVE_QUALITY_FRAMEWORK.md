# Narrative quality framework

## Narrative goals

The story must make the player's driving choices emotionally and systemically meaningful without turning the game into a dialogue-heavy visual novel.

## Quality criteria

- Characters have distinct goals, voices, and relationships.
- Factions are understandable without being reduced to simple good/evil labels.
- Major decisions have immediate, delayed, and systemic consequences.
- Dialogue is concise and context-sensitive.
- The world communicates history through spaces, props, radio, and events.
- Endings reflect accumulated behavior rather than one arbitrary final choice.
- Humor and darkness reinforce the fictional tone rather than trivializing real-world suffering.

## Narrative testing

Ask players to summarize each faction, explain their own choices, identify who benefited from them, and predict at least one consequence. Confusion about causality is a design defect, not merely a writing preference.
