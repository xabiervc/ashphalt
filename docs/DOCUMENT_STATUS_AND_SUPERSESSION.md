# Document status and supersession

## Status labels

- **Normative:** defines approved product/system rules.
- **Supporting:** explains intent or gives examples; cannot override normative rules.
- **Operational:** defines exact implementation/test content for an approved scope.
- **Deferred:** intentionally outside current implementation.
- **Superseded:** retained for history but must not guide new work.
- **Experimental:** hypothesis under test; never assume production truth.

## Current rule

The latest repository documents are not automatically authoritative by filename or date. Authority comes from `CANONICAL_PREPRODUCTION_INDEX.md` and the status recorded here.

## Required metadata

Every future specialist document should begin with status, owner, scope, dependencies, and last review date.

## Archive policy

Historical documents are retained for traceability. They are not deleted merely to hide evolution; they are marked Superseded when replaced.
