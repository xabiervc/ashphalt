# Art direction and mood

## Visual thesis

Ashphalt shows a world that is damaged but alive: civic systems have failed, yet people improvise, trade, compete, repair, and celebrate. The style should combine believable material wear with bold arcade readability.

## Key principles

- Strong silhouettes for vehicles and Authority units.
- Clear faction color/value language without color-only communication.
- Destruction that reads at driving speed.
- Distinctive skyline, material, and lighting identity for each city.
- Controlled visual noise: spectacle must not hide objectives or hazards.
- Stylized pedestrians and fictional signage.

## Vertical-slice mood

The Crown uses wet black asphalt, sodium light, broken high-rises, red Authority beacons, improvised neon, and ash drifting through the air. The player should remember its silhouette after one session.

## Asset acceptance

Every final asset must have a gameplay purpose, damage/LOD policy, performance budget, source/license record, and accessibility/readability review.
