# Police and emergency catalog

## Authority power fantasy

Authority vehicles must be meaningfully more powerful than ordinary traffic and most early rivals. Their threat comes from coordination, mass, equipment, and escalation — not merely inflated hit points.

## Units

| Unit | Role | Mass | Armor | Top speed | Threat | Unlock/trigger |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---|---|
| Patrol bike | scout/blocker | low | 120 | high | tracking, route marking | low heat |
| Interceptor | pursuit | medium | 450 | very high | side-hit, tracking | medium heat |
| Heavy rammer | capture | high | 1,200 | medium | frontal impact, pinning | high heat |
| Roadblock van | control | very high | 1,800 | low | route denial, barriers | high heat |
| Drone support | reconnaissance | none | 80 | n/a | reveals route/player | elite heat |
| Command unit | elite pursuit | high | 2,500 | high | coordinated spawns, EMP-style disruption | story/elite event |

Values are initial balance targets, not final truth.

## Escalation

Heat tiers: 0 none, 1 observation, 2 patrol, 3 interceptor, 4 heavy response, 5 roadblock/command response. Heat decays only when the player breaks line of sight and avoids new incidents.

## Emergency units

Fire and ambulance equivalents are normally neutral. They navigate to accident events, create temporary obstacles, and can become protected objectives. A dedicated event may make them hostile only through explicit story or faction context.

## AI requirements

Authority uses perception, pursuit prediction, road blocking, backup requests, recovery, and disengagement. Heavy units should be powerful but readable, with warning audio, lights, route markers, and telegraphed arrival.
