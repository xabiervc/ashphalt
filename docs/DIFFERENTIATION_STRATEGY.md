# Differentiation strategy

## Ashphalt is not

- A licensed-car collection racer.
- A realistic driving simulator.
- A conventional open-world crime game.
- A pure destruction arena.
- A linear checkpoint time trial.
- A live-service progression treadmill.

## Ashphalt is

An open-city destruction race where a persistent Chaos Rating changes cities, a road-movie campaign makes travel meaningful, vehicles carry strategic identities, and speed/destruction/protection choices shape who controls the infrastructure that survives.

## Proof in the first slice

The Crown must demonstrate that leaving the race route, changing city state, helping or betraying a faction, and surviving a powerful Authority response create different playable outcomes.
