# Open-world vertical slice

The first vertical slice is an explorable section of The Crown with five logical zones: Financial Ruins, Old Transit, Civic Spire, Flooded Underground, and Workshop Row.

It must support continuous traversal, one official race, one shortcut, one free-roam activity, one rival encounter, Authority pursuit, one weather route change, a faction decision, a persistent city-state transition, and visible aftermath. Main-route traversal targets 4-7 minutes; free exploration targets at least 10 minutes without mandatory loading.

A standalone closed track is insufficient for Q3.
