# Canon rules

## Fixed canon

- Ashphalt: Aftermath is fictional and single-player-first.
- The Ash Race crosses five major open cities.
- The Fire Key is technological infrastructure authorization, not magic.
- The Gate is a physical industrial installation.
- Chaos Rating and faction reputation are separate systems.
- Cities have persistent states up to Ash State.

## Flexible canon

Exact pre-collapse history, minor character biographies, city population numbers, and optional radio rumors may change if they do not contradict fixed canon.

## Prohibited contradictions

Do not turn The Fire Key into an omnipotent weapon, remove player agency through hidden choices, erase city-state consequences without an authored recovery event, or present real-world tragedies as direct spectacle.

## Canon review

Every new story asset records which fixed rules it uses, which flexible assumptions it adds, and which existing flags it can change.
