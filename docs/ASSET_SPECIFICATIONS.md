# Asset specifications

## First playable placeholder assets

- One drivable vehicle mesh with collision and four wheel bones.
- One rival vehicle placeholder.
- Modular road, curb, wall, barrier, workshop, and building blocks.
- Damage placeholder meshes for body, wheel, bumper, and glass.
- Three pedestrian silhouettes.
- One Authority patrol placeholder.
- Weather placeholder particles for rain, fog, ash, and debris.
- UI icons for damage, heat, score, combo, objective, faction, and repair.

## Vertical slice asset targets

- 5 final-quality vehicle bodies.
- 3 district kits.
- 20 modular environment kits.
- 8 character models or stylized representations.
- 7 NPC categories.
- 6 Authority/emergency vehicles.
- 12 VFX families.
- 30 audio interaction groups.
- 2 music themes plus event variations.

## Asset metadata

Every asset has ID, owner, source/license, target platform, memory budget, LOD policy, collision policy, material slots, damage variants, localization/audio dependencies, and approval state.
