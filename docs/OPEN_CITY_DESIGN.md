# Open-city design

## Core decision

Ashphalt's cities are open, explorable driving spaces. Races are activities inside the city, not isolated tracks. The player can leave a route, become lost, discover shortcuts, encounter rivals, find workshops, trigger events, and return to the objective.

## City composition

Each major city targets approximately five subdistricts with distinct route grammar, landmark language, hazards, factions, and activities. The target is density and identity, not maximum square kilometres.

## Player verbs

Drive, race, drift, ram, evade, explore, discover, repair, tune, salvage, rescue, sabotage, negotiate, assist, challenge, and return.

## Loading and continuity

The first vertical slice should support at least ten minutes of free-roam driving without a mandatory loading screen. Streaming boundaries must preserve city-state, traffic, rival, weather, and objective context.

## Quality rule

A larger map is not automatically better. Every expandable area must have a purpose, route variety, landmark, activity, or consequence. Empty filler is a production defect.
