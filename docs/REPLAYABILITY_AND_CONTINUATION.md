# Replayability and continuation

## Why continue after failure

Failure preserves learning, discoveries, some resources, rival memory, and story context. A failed event may change the next route or relationship instead of merely restarting the same state.

## Why replay

- Alternate vehicle archetypes and builds.
- Multiple routes and weather seeds.
- Different rival lineups and behavior.
- Chaos Rating mastery.
- Faction paths and endings.
- City-state recovery versus destruction.
- Optional activities and hidden consequences.
- Difficulty/component settings.

## Duration target

25-35 hours for a campaign-focused playthrough; 50+ hours for broad completion and mastery is an aspirational target subject to playtesting. No artificial grinding is required for the main story.
