# First-hour learning curve

## Skills learned

- 0-5 minutes: basic controls, recovery, workshop, route choice.
- 5-15 minutes: braking, cornering, shortcuts, collision consequences.
- 15-25 minutes: Chaos Rating, rival targeting, Authority heat, event rewards.
- 25-40 minutes: tuning trade-offs, repair timing, faction choice, free-roam discovery.
- 40-60 minutes: weather route change, rival behavior, city-state consequence, deliberate risk planning.

## First-hour decision

The player must make one choice that distinguishes Ashphalt from ordinary racing: prioritize speed, destruction, protection, or exploration, knowing that it affects the city and future access.

## Acceptance

At least 80% of test participants can explain one vehicle trade-off, one route trade-off, one faction consequence, and one reason to continue after failure.
