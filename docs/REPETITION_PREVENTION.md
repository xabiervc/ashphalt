# Repetition prevention

## Variation dimensions

- Route choice.
- Weather seed.
- Rival lineup and target behavior.
- City state.
- Faction contract.
- Optional objective.
- Traffic density.
- Police heat.
- Vehicle build.
- Rescue/sabotage decision.

## Rule

A repeated event must change at least two meaningful dimensions or be presented as a mastery challenge with a clear reason to replay.

## Content audit

For every event record its unique verb, route identity, narrative purpose, systemic modifier, reward decision, and replay reason. Events failing two or more fields are candidates for removal or combination.
