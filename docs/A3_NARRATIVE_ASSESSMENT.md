# A3 — Narrative assessment

## Closed decisions

- The narrative premise, player role, world frame, campaign intent, key factions, event structure, consequences, canon boundaries, and relationship to gameplay systems are defined in the narrative documents.
- The narrative is not treated as disconnected flavour: player choices, reputation, heat, city state, and aftermath have specified hooks.

## Prototype boundary

Final emotional impact, dialogue pacing, and voice/audio delivery require a playable slice and review.
