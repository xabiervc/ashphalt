# Prototype acceptance tests

## Traversal

- Player can drive through all five logical zones without a loading screen.
- Main route completes in the target 4-7 minute range after tuning.
- Player can leave the race route and return without losing event state.
- Recovery guidance works without forced teleportation.

## Navigation

- Workshop, Crown Needle, shortcut, Authority Arch, and Floodgate Nine are discoverable.
- Optional map and objective guidance remain functional.
- Landmark descriptions and critical indicators are not color-only.

## Gameplay

- One official race, one destruction event, one rival encounter, and The Stray Signal activity work end to end.
- Damage changes vehicle behavior and repair restores it.
- Authority escalation is readable and avoidable through player decisions.
- Weather changes one route and visibly affects driving.
- City-state consequence persists after leaving and returning.

## Quality

- Q1 handling test passes.
- Baseline accessibility settings persist.
- Save/load works at workshop, during exploration, after event, and at cell boundary.
- Replay scenario stays within documented tolerances.
- Performance capture is attached to the milestone report.
