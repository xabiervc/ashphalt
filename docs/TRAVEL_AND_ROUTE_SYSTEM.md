# Travel and route system

## Purpose

Travel between cities is playable decision-making, not a passive loading screen and not a tedious survival simulator.

## Route choice

Before each stage, the player chooses from available routes with visible risk indicators:

- Fast route: shorter, higher Authority attention.
- Safe route: longer, more traffic and lower rewards.
- Salvage route: parts opportunity, ambush risk.
- Faction route: reputation requirement and faction-specific consequence.
- Weather route: affected by current forecast and vehicle setup.

## Travel events

- Rival challenge.
- Convoy request.
- Emergency distress call.
- Workshop opportunity.
- Roadblock.
- Weather front.
- Salvage cache.
- Information trade.
- Faction ambush.
- Shortcut discovery.

## Anti-tedium rules

Travel events are short, skippable where safe, and never require repetitive fuel or hunger management. A route choice must offer a meaningful trade-off, not a disguised chore.

## Persistent route state

Record route damage, blocked paths, faction control, discovered shortcuts, emergency consequences, weather seed, and player reputation. A city can be revisited in a different Ash State.

## Acceptance criteria

Players can predict the broad risk/reward of a route, understand why it changed, and feel that their choice matters in the next event.
