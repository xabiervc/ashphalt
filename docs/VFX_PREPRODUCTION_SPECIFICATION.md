# VFX pre-production specification

## VFX families

- Sparks, smoke, fire, debris, glass, body fragments, tire smoke.
- Rain, fog, ash, sand, water spray, wind debris.
- Authority lights, warning markers, drones, barriers, pursuit effects.
- Faction signage, route discovery, Chaos Rating, city-state transition.
- Workshop repair, upgrade installation, Fire Key technology, The Gate.

## Readability rules

VFX must communicate cause, severity, duration, ownership, and gameplay effect. Effects may not obscure the driving line, objective, or critical threat.

## Budget classes

Every effect is tagged small, medium, large, or set-piece with particle count, GPU/CPU cost, lights, translucency, sound events, LOD policy, and platform fallback.

## Acceptance

Run effects at target traffic, destruction, weather, and Authority density. Record frame impact and verify that players can still identify the road and objective.
