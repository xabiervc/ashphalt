# A2 — Gameplay assessment

## Closed decisions

- The minute-to-minute loop is specified from preparation through traversal, risk, decision, consequence, recovery, and progression.
- Player actions, inputs, feedback, objectives, failure, recovery, challenge escalation, and progression rules are documented.
- Core variables have owners, ranges or initial hypotheses, dependencies, and acceptance checks.

## Reviewer test

A programmer can implement a first controlled prototype without deciding what the player is meant to do or how the principal systems relate.

## Prototype boundary

Driving feel, exact balance, camera comfort, and fun remain runtime hypotheses.
