# Quality risk register

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation | Gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Vehicle physics feels generic | fatal to identity | repeated blind tests, handling prototypes | Q1 |
| Chaos physics diverges | replay/network risk | tolerances, checkpoints, explicit limitations | Q4 |
| Destruction becomes visual noise | weak strategy | readable damage states and objective feedback | Q2 |
| Police overwhelms the player | frustration | telegraphing, escalation tuning, recovery routes | Q2 |
| Faction consequences are invisible | narrative failure | UI reason codes and playtest prediction tests | Q3 |
| Pedestrian content causes discomfort | rating/accessibility risk | stylization, non-contact mode, content review | Q3 |
| Scope overwhelms production | schedule failure | vertical-slice gate and content matrix | every gate |
| AI traffic hurts performance | technical failure | profiling, density budgets, Mass evaluation | Q4 |
| Narrative branches become inconsistent | QA failure | decision matrix, automated ending tests | Q4 |
| Accessibility is late | expensive rework | options in first playable, persistent settings tests | Q2 |
