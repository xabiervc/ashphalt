# Mixing and audio accessibility

## Mix priorities

1. Player control feedback.
2. Immediate collision/damage feedback.
3. Threat and Authority warnings.
4. Objective and consequence feedback.
5. Dialogue/radio.
6. Music.
7. Environmental ambience.

## Dynamic mix

Use ducking by priority, distance, city state, and camera context. Avoid compressing all audio into a constant wall of noise. Preserve transient impact while protecting hearing comfort.

## Options

Independent volume controls, mono output, dynamic range presets, subtitle/caption settings, visual threat indicators, reduced alarm intensity, and visual equivalents for critical audio cues.

## Tests

Validate with music off, effects off, dialogue off, mono output, low volume, headphones, speakers, and accessibility indicators enabled.
