# Localization and content rules

## Languages

Prepare localization keys from the beginning. Initial source language: English. Spanish should be a first localization target given the project's origin and expected audience.

## Text rules

Avoid text baked into textures. Use stable IDs, pluralization-aware strings, context notes, maximum lengths, and fallback values.

## Content boundaries

The game is fictional and stylized. Avoid real-world political groups, identifiable tragedies, real brands, and realistic depictions of current disasters. Review violence, pedestrian presentation, accessibility, and platform rating requirements before release.

## Audio

Use original or properly licensed music and sound. Store cue IDs and alternate intensity versions rather than embedding irreversible references in gameplay code.
