# First playable content lock

## Mandatory

- One open Crown subdistrict with five logical zones.
- One drivable vehicle with control metrics.
- One rival and one Authority interceptor.
- One main race, one shortcut, one free-roam activity.
- Damage, workshop, three upgrades, score, combo, heat, reputation.
- One weather route change.
- One faction decision and city-state consequence.
- HUD, accessibility baseline, save/load, recoverable errors, debug commands.
- Track A/B/C evidence as applicable to the gate.

## Aspirational and excluded

Additional cities, full campaign, final music/voice, dense crowds, multiplayer, final cinematics, and broad asset production are excluded until the locked slice passes Q3.

## Completion

The lock is complete when the content list, asset list, test list, duration target, performance budget, and owner list are all filled with specific values.
