# Core gameplay specification

## Product sentence

Ashphalt: Aftermath is an open-city arcade vehicular-combat racing game in which a reckless driver navigates dense post-collapse cities, turns speed and destruction into score and leverage, and makes choices that permanently change routes, factions, rivals, and city states.

## Minute-to-minute loop

1. Read objective, route, threat, vehicle condition, and reward.
2. Choose a line: official route, shortcut, attack, escape, exploration, or rescue.
3. Drive with weighty but responsive arcade controls.
4. Convert risk into speed, score, time, damage, heat, and reputation.
5. Recover from mistakes through handling, route choice, repair, or fallback objectives.
6. Read the consequence and decide whether to continue, repair, explore, or launch another event.

## Core decisions

- Speed versus destruction.
- Safe route versus shortcut.
- Attack rival versus preserve vehicle.
- Continue damaged versus repair.
- Maximize Chaos Rating versus protect city infrastructure.
- Follow objective guidance versus explore.
- Accept faction advantage versus preserve independence.

## Success test

A new player should make at least three of these decisions in the first 10 minutes without needing a design explanation.
