# Technical quality budgets

The Crown prototype must measure frame, physics, AI, streaming, draw-call, memory, and collision-event budgets at target traffic and Authority density. A ten-minute free-roam benchmark must include at least one cell crossing, weather transition, rival encounter, damage event, workshop return, and save/load.

No density, map-size, or visual-fidelity expansion is approved without a benchmark capture and a review of player readability.
