# Accessibility targets

## Controls

- Full remapping for keyboard, mouse, and controller.
- Separate throttle, brake, steering, camera, handbrake, reset, and power-up bindings.
- Toggle/hold alternatives.
- Steering sensitivity, dead zones, vibration, camera shake, motion blur, and field-of-view controls.
- Optional assisted steering and braking for onboarding.

## Visual and audio

- Subtitles with speaker identification and adjustable size.
- Color-safe faction, damage, objective, and heat indicators.
- Shape/icon redundancy; never communicate critical state by color alone.
- High-contrast HUD option.
- Reduced flashes, particles, camera shake, and motion effects.
- Independent music, dialogue, engine, effects, and UI volume.
- Visual indicators for important audio cues.

## Cognitive and motor

- Adjustable difficulty and pursuit intensity.
- Pause during single-player gameplay.
- Objective reminders and route guidance.
- Slow-motion or relaxed input assistance as an optional accessibility mode.
- Non-contact pedestrian mode with equivalent progression.
- No mandatory rapid button sequences.
- Recoverable failures and clear restart options.

## Accessibility acceptance

Every option must be tested by turning it on, saving it, restarting, and confirming it remains active and actually changes the relevant experience.
