# District catalog

| ID | District | Gameplay identity | Main hazards | Faction pressure |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D_CROWN | The Crown | dense urban circuit | checkpoints, tunnels, vertical shortcuts | Authority |
| D_WORKS | The Works | industrial combat | containers, cranes, toxic runoff | Underworld |
| D_VERGE | The Verge | suburban route network | narrow alleys, convoy traffic | Settlements |
| D_SALT | The Salt Run | high-speed off-road | sandstorms, debris, long sightlines | Circuit |
| D_LOWLANDS | The Lowlands | flood-changing routes | water, collapses, blocked roads | mixed |

Every district requires a workshop, primary loop, alternate route, faction event, emergency space, benchmark zone, and safe recovery area.
