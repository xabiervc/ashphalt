# Characters and relationships

The core cast participates in the seven-stage Ash Race season. Character states include trust, debt, fear, respect, faction snapshot, relationship tier, available scenes/contracts, and exit state. Rival behavior and dialogue must respond to player destruction, rescues, betrayals, city-state transitions, and decisions.

The full production roster and test expectations are in `RIVAL_DRIVER_SPECIFICATION.md`, `NARRATIVE_DATA_CONTRACTS.md`, and `CAMPAIGN_QA_MATRIX.md`.
