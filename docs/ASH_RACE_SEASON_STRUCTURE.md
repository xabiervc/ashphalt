# Ash Race season structure

## Campaign format

The campaign is a season of five city stages plus a prologue and finale. Each stage contains a required qualifier, optional events, a rival confrontation, a city-state escalation, and a departure decision.

## Stage order

1. Starting Line — tutorial and first Fire Key clue.
2. The Crown — Authority control and urban checkpoints.
3. The Works — Underworld salvage and industrial destruction.
4. The Verge — settlements, convoys, and civilian consequences.
5. The Salt Run — long-distance routes, sandstorms, and rival alliances.
6. The Lowlands — floods, emergency response, and infrastructure collapse.
7. The Gate — finale and Fire Key decision.

## Season progression

Each stage unlocks vehicles, parts, routes, story flags, and rival states. A player may complete optional events in different orders, but the main progression must never become unclear.

## Quality requirements

Every stage has a unique gameplay verb, a unique visual motif, a signature rival beat, a route decision, a persistent city-state change, and a recoverable failure state.
