# Traffic and AI

See `NPC_CATALOG.md` and `POLICE_AND_EMERGENCY_CATALOG.md` for initial NPC and Authority unit catalogs.

AI groups include civilian traffic, rival racers, Authority units, Underworld ambushers, Settlement escorts, emergency responders, and stylized pedestrians. Every group needs deterministic scenarios for normal behavior, collision, blocked routes, weather, pursuit, repair, and reputation response.
