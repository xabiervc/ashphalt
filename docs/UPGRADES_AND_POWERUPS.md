## Consumable power-ups

- Emergency repair: restores 12% of the most damaged component.
- Impact surge: increases ramming impulse 35% for 6 seconds.
- Grip burst: increases grip 18% for 5 seconds.
- Decoy flare: causes pursuit target confusion for 4 seconds.
- Route pulse: reveals nearby shortcut or hazard for 8 seconds.
- Traffic jammer: causes civilian traffic to pull over for 5 seconds.

Every power-up has duration, cooldown, counterplay, audio/visual telegraph, inventory limit, and deterministic spawn rule.
