# Implementation data backlog

## Content required for first playable

- 1 district blockout.
- 1 player vehicle asset or placeholder.
- 1 rival vehicle placeholder.
- 1 workshop.
- 1 race route and shortcut.
- 1 Authority patrol placeholder.
- 1 reputation event and consequence.
- HUD showing objective, score, combo, heat, damage, timer, and reward.

## Content required for vertical slice

- 5 vehicle archetypes.
- 3 districts.
- 4 faction data sets.
- 8 named characters.
- 10 event templates.
- 6 weather states.
- 6 Authority/emergency unit types.
- 7 pedestrian types.
- 30 upgrades/power-ups.
- 2-3 major story decisions.

## Data validation

Every asset must pass ID uniqueness, required-field, range, compatibility, localization-key, save-version, and deterministic-seed validation before entering a build.
