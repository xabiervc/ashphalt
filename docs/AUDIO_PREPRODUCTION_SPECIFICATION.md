# Audio pre-production specification

## Audio pillars

1. Driving must feel physical through engine load, tires, impacts, body damage, and surface changes.
2. Threats must be identifiable without relying on visuals.
3. Each city and faction must have an audible identity.
4. Chaos should escalate musically and sonically without causing fatigue.
5. Audio options must make critical information available through redundant visual cues.

## Music system

Music has four layers: exploration, race, high-chaos, and aftermath. Layers transition by state rather than restarting randomly. Each city has a motif; each faction has an accent; The Ash Race has a recurring signature.

Initial music brief:

- Exploration: damaged industrial ambience, restrained pulse, environmental rhythm.
- Race: aggressive drums, distorted bass, forward momentum.
- High Chaos: denser percussion, alarm-like textures, controlled intensity.
- Aftermath: sparse harmonic material, radio fragments, consequence emphasis.

## Music catalog target

- 5 city motifs.
- 4 faction accents.
- 1 Ash Race theme.
- 6 intensity layers.
- 8 aftermath/decision cues.
- 10 menu/workshop/UI cues.

Use original or properly licensed music only. Store cue IDs, stems, loop points, intensity state, mix priority, and localization/credit metadata.

## SFX catalog target

- 5 vehicle archetype engine families.
- 8 tire/surface families.
- 12 collision/material families.
- 6 damage-state families.
- 6 Authority/emergency families.
- 6 weather families.
- 5 city ambience families.
- 4 UI/faction feedback families.

## Acceptance

Players can identify throttle load, severe damage, nearby Authority threat, route hazard, event completion, and city-state escalation with visual support disabled where feasible.
