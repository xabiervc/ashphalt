# Player fantasy and genre

## Exact genre

Single-player open-city arcade vehicular-combat racing game with road-movie campaign structure, systemic destruction, exploration, faction reputation, and consequence-driven narrative.

## Player fantasy

Become the most notorious driver in a broken world: drive faster than the collapse, turn cities into dangerous playgrounds, outsmart powerful Authority units, build a vehicle that reflects your style, and decide whether your legend leaves freedom, reconstruction, exploitation, or ashes behind.

## Subgenre references

The game combines arcade racing, vehicular combat, open-world exploration, systemic sandbox events, and branching campaign consequences. It is not a simulator, pure demolition derby, traditional open-world crime game, or linear cinematic racer.

## Anti-confusion rule

Every feature must support speed, destruction, exploration, preparation, consequence, or readable spectacle. If it supports none, defer or cut it.
