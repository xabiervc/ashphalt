# The Crown open-district prototype

## Purpose

Prove that Ashphalt's core experience works in an explorable district rather than on an isolated track.

## Playable footprint

Use one compact but continuous district composed of five logical zones:

1. Financial Ruins: main boulevard, tower canyon, primary race start.
2. Old Transit: tunnel, station platforms, maintenance shortcut.
3. Civic Spire: Authority checkpoint, plaza, pursuit arena.
4. Flooded Underground: lower route, emergency objective, weather closure.
5. Workshop Row: player garage, salvage yard, free-roam challenge.

The prototype may use blockout geometry. It must have continuous roads, readable boundaries, recovery loops, and no dead-end route that traps the player.

## Required loop

Spawn at Workshop Row → choose an event → drive freely → discover or follow a route → encounter a rival or activity → complete a race or destruction objective → receive damage/reward/reputation → return to workshop or continue exploring → observe one changed district consequence.

## Required content

- One player vehicle.
- One rival vehicle.
- One Authority interceptor.
- Civilian traffic placeholders.
- Stylized pedestrian placeholders or non-contact mode.
- One workshop.
- One landmark per logical zone.
- One official circuit route.
- One discovered shortcut.
- One free-roam activity.
- One weather-driven route change.
- One city-state transition.
- One faction decision.

## Scale target

A first traversal using the main road should take 4-7 minutes. Free exploration should support at least 10 minutes without mandatory loading. The district must feel dense, not empty; the final dimensions are subject to vehicle-speed testing.
