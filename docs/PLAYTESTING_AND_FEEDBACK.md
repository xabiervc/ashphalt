# Playtesting and feedback

## Method

Use repeated small tests rather than one late focus group. Recruit players with varied racing experience, accessibility needs, and familiarity with destruction games.

## Test sessions

- 10-minute handling test.
- 20-minute first-playable loop.
- 45-minute vertical-slice session.
- Return-session test after at least 48 hours.
- Accessibility-specific sessions with the relevant options enabled.

## Capture

Record completion, deaths/failures, route choices, collisions, repair use, score actions, heat response, confusion points, time to first success, voluntary replay, verbal explanations, and post-session ratings.

## Success signals

- Players understand the objective without coaching.
- Players can explain why a vehicle is damaged.
- Players discover at least one strategic use of destruction.
- Players notice faction consequences without needing a spreadsheet.
- Players voluntarily try a second vehicle or event.
- Players describe a memorable moment in their own words.

## Feedback discipline

Separate preference from usability failure. Tag findings as blocker, systemic, balance, content, polish, accessibility, or personal preference. Never implement every suggestion automatically; preserve the product pillars.
