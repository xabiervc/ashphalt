# Implementation readiness report

## Ready now

- Product identity and genre.
- Core player fantasy and loop.
- Measurable driving hypotheses.
- Open Crown prototype scope.
- Race/challenge/progression tracks.
- Narrative canon and state model.
- Vehicle/damage/tuning/repair rules.
- Faction, city-state, scoring, AI, weather, audio, VFX, animation, accessibility, save, QA, and release requirements.
- Scope freeze and cut rules.

## Not yet proven

- Vehicle feel.
- Fun and retention.
- Final balance.
- Performance on target hardware.
- Streaming smoothness.
- Accessibility effectiveness with users.
- Audio/VFX mix quality.
- Narrative comprehension.

## Readiness decision

Proceed with controlled prototype implementation. Do not begin full production until the Crown slice passes its gates with evidence.
