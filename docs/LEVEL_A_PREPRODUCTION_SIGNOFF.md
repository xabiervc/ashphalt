# Level-A pre-production sign-off

## Classification

**Ashphalt is Level A for controlled prototype implementation.**

This means the team can begin Track A, Track B, and the locked Crown slice without resolving fundamental product or structural design questions during implementation.

It does not mean the game is implemented, balanced, fun, performant, accessible in practice, or ready for full production.

## Required evidence before full production

- Track A passes Q1 handling.
- Track B passes route/challenge clarity.
- The Crown slice passes Q2/Q3.
- Save/load, replay, accessibility, performance, audio/VFX placeholder, and streaming tests produce evidence.
- Open risks have owners and decisions.
- External playtest feedback is incorporated.

## Sign-off criteria

- Product canon is frozen.
- Mandatory prototype scope is frozen.
- Every critical requirement has a document, data/state target, scene/asset target, test, and gate.
- Deferred and aspirational work cannot silently enter the prototype.
- Conflicting documents are classified or resolved.
