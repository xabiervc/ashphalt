# Character arcs

## Mara Venn

Mechanic and settlement bridge. Starts as practical mentor, challenges the player's destructive choices, and can become coalition leader, estranged critic, or final workshop ally.

## Marshal Dorne

Authority commander. Starts as pursuer, offers conditional order, and can become negotiator, authoritarian final opponent, or reluctant protector.

## Ivo Krail

Underworld broker. Offers performance and information, accumulates debt, and may become supplier, betrayer, or casualty of the player's choices.

## Sera Quill

Circuit champion. Begins as rival benchmark, responds to fair competition or cruelty, and can become rival ally, final competitor, or public enemy.

## Niko Vale

Route scout and information trader. Changes loyalty according to trust, payment, fear, and city state; can reveal or misdirect routes.

## Arc acceptance

Each arc has introduction, escalation, decision, consequence, recovery, and ending contribution. Every arc must be testable without requiring one exact order of optional events.
