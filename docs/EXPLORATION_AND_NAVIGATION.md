# Exploration and navigation

## Navigation philosophy

The player should be allowed to get lost, but not abandoned. Discovery must reward observation and experimentation while providing recovery tools.

## Guidance layers

- Large diegetic landmarks.
- Faction lighting, smoke, radio, signage, and convoy movement.
- Optional map and route markers.
- Compass and objective direction.
- NPC route hints.
- Discovered shortcut markers.
- Safehouse/workshop recall.

## Lost-player recovery

After a configurable period without progress, offer a subtle radio hint, map pulse, nearby landmark highlight, or optional route reset. Never teleport the player without consent except in a clear fail state.

## Exploration rewards

Discoveries include workshops, shortcuts, hidden events, vehicle parts, lore recordings, rival encounters, emergency opportunities, and faction information. Rewards should alter future decisions rather than only provide collectibles.

## Acceptance tests

Players can reach an objective using the route marker, can intentionally navigate without it using landmarks, and understand how to recover after getting lost.
