# Exposition and pacing rules

## Rules

- Show systems through driving before explaining them.
- Deliver essential information through at least two channels.
- Keep radio lines short during high-speed play.
- Do not repeat the same tutorial explanation after the player has demonstrated understanding.
- Use workshops, rival encounters, landmarks, and aftermaths to expose world information naturally.
- Every exposition beat must change a decision, unlock context, or reframe a character.

## Test

After a session, ask players to explain the Fire Key, the current faction conflict, their last consequence, and their next objective. Confusion must produce a revision ticket.
