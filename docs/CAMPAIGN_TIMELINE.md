# Campaign timeline

## Before the race

Infrastructure collapse fragments the old network. The Fire Key is divided, hidden, or inaccessible. Factions begin rebuilding isolated corridors.

## Starting Line

The player repairs a vehicle, receives the invitation, and learns that a missing contact knew about The Fire Key.

## The Crown

Authority checkpoints and manipulated race routes reveal that the Ash Race is selecting targets, not merely following roads.

## The Works

Underworld workshops reveal that vehicle parts and race sabotage are connected to the old network.

## The Verge

Settlement consequences force the player to choose between spectacle and reconstruction.

## The Salt Run

Rivals form alliances while the player learns The Circuit is not the true organizer.

## The Lowlands

Emergency infrastructure and Fire Key access converge. The player learns The Gate can reconnect cities or centralize control.

## The Gate

The player chooses who, if anyone, controls The Fire Key.

Every timeline beat has a stage ID, required flags, optional flags, trigger event, fallback, and test scenario.
