# First five minutes

## Player experience

0:00-0:45 — Start at Workshop Row, inspect a damaged vehicle, choose a basic repair, and receive the first Ash Race invitation.

0:45-2:00 — Drive through Financial Ruins with throttle, brake, steering, handbrake, camera, and recovery introduced through play rather than a lecture.

2:00-3:15 — Encounter a route fork: safe boulevard or risky Old Transit shortcut. The player sees a landmark and chooses.

3:15-4:20 — A rival appears, causes a collision, and escapes. The player learns that damage is functional and that pursuit can be avoided or confronted.

4:20-5:00 — Reach the first event marker, see objective/risk/reward/Chaos Rating, and choose whether to start or explore. The player receives a Fire Key clue through radio/environmental context.

## Acceptance

A new player can describe the basic objective, name one risk/reward choice, identify the workshop, and explain why their vehicle was damaged without coaching.
