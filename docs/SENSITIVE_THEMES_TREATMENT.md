# Sensitive themes treatment

## Themes

Collapse, displacement, authority, coercion, exploitation, public spectacle, death, destruction, and rebuilding.

## Treatment rules

Keep the setting fictional and stylized. Avoid identifiable real tragedies, current political groups, real emergency symbols used deceptively, or realistic instructional depictions of terrorism. The game may be provocative, but consequences must be framed as fictional systems and character choices.

Violent spectacle must not erase civilian agency. Settlements, emergency workers, and affected communities have goals, voices, and recovery states.

## Player controls

Provide a non-contact pedestrian mode, reduced intensity options, warnings where appropriate, and clear content settings. Review final presentation against target ratings before release.
