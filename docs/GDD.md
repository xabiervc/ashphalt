# Ashphalt: Aftermath — product brief

## Fixed canon

Ashphalt: Aftermath is a single-player-first open-city arcade vehicular-combat racing game. The player enters The Ash Race, travels through five fictional open cities, converts speed and destruction into score and leverage, and decides what their legend leaves behind.

The central technology is The Fire Key, an old-world authorization core capable of restarting or controlling surviving energy and communication corridors. The final installation is The Gate. Chaos Rating and faction reputation are separate. Cities persist in states up to Ash State.
