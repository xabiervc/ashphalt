# Ashphalt: Aftermath — Game Design Document

## Canon

- Game: **Ashphalt: Aftermath**.
- Competition: **The Ash Race**.
- Core technology: **The Fire Key**.
- Final destination: **The Gate**.
- Destruction system: **Chaos Rating**.
- Maximum city destruction state: **Ash State**.

Ashphalt: Aftermath is a single-player-first post-apocalyptic arcade racing and vehicular-combat game. It combines aggressive driving, vehicle destruction, repair and tuning, faction politics, dynamic weather, a multi-city road journey, and consequence-driven narrative.

The player joins The Ash Race, a clandestine competition whose pilots travel through isolated cities and deliberately turn them into stages of escalating chaos. The race is not only about reaching the finish line: players earn fame and rewards through speed, destruction, survival, stunts, rival defeats, police encounters, and choices about whom to protect or betray.

The Fire Key is an old-world authorization core capable of restarting or controlling the surviving energy and communications corridors. The winner is promised access to it, but the player's behavior determines who can use it and whether the cities remain connected or become another battlefield.

## Quality target

Develop toward a standard capable of competing for recognition in categories represented by The Game Awards, D.I.C.E. Awards, BAFTA Games Awards, and Game Developers Choice Awards. This is an aspiration and measurable framework, not a prediction of awards.

Every city stage must provide a distinctive gameplay identity, a memorable set piece, multiple viable strategies, a named rival turn, visible systemic consequences, accessibility options, and a fail-forward state.
