# Ashphalt — Game Design Document

> Quality target: develop toward a standard capable of competing for recognition in categories represented by The Game Awards, D.I.C.E. Awards, BAFTA Games Awards, and Game Developers Choice Awards. This is an aspiration and a measurable quality framework, not a claim of likely nomination or victory.

Ashphalt is a single-player-first post-apocalyptic arcade racing and vehicular-combat game combining reckless driving, vehicle destruction, repair, tuning, faction politics, dynamic weather, and consequence-driven narrative.

The product-level design is expanded by the specialist documents in `docs/`. Those documents are authoritative for their domains and must be implemented with evidence, testing, and playtest validation rather than treated as promises.

## Quality standard

Every major system must have a player-facing purpose, acceptance criteria, test scenario, playtest result, known-risk decision, and polish pass. The first vertical slice must be memorable after one session and communicate what makes Ashphalt distinct.

## Scope

Fictional cities, fictional vehicles, single-player first. Local and online multiplayer remain deferred until the core game is stable, accessible, performant, and fun.
