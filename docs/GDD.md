# Ashphalt: Aftermath — Game Design Document

## Core playable definition

Ashphalt: Aftermath is a single-player open-city arcade vehicular-combat racing game with a road-movie campaign. The player drives through dense fictional post-collapse cities, races and destroys through The Ash Race, explores outside official routes, builds a vehicle with meaningful trade-offs, and changes factions, rivals, routes, and city states through play.

The central fantasy is: **become the most notorious driver in a broken world, then decide what your legend leaves behind.**

The core minute-to-minute loop is defined in `CORE_GAMEPLAY_SPECIFICATION.md`. The measurable control, camera, collision, race-flow, difficulty, progression, differentiation, and prototype plans are now defined in the corresponding specialist documents.

All numerical values are prototype hypotheses until validated in Unreal and playtests. The first unknown to resolve is not more content; it is whether the vehicle feels precise, weighty, readable, and replayable.
