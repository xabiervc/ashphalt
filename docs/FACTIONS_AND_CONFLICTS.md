# Factions and conflicts

## The Authority

Goal: restore centralized order and control roads, checkpoints, data, and emergency resources.

Methods: permits, patrols, confiscation, surveillance, selective protection.

Player relationship: the Authority can reduce random hostility and open secure routes, but expects obedience and may restrict black-market tuning.

## The Underworld

Goal: control salvage, illegal parts, workshops, and information.

Methods: smuggling, ambushes, sabotage, debt, exclusive upgrades.

Player relationship: offers performance and shortcuts at the cost of instability, enemies, and betrayal risk.

## The Settlements

Goal: protect civilians, restore utilities, and keep routes safe enough for rebuilding.

Methods: escorts, community races, repairs, barricades, mutual aid.

Player relationship: rewards restraint, protection, and delivery work, but may oppose indiscriminate destruction.

## The Circuit

Goal: dominate the cultural and economic power of racing.

Methods: rival crews, spectacle, sponsorship, dangerous challenges, public reputation.

Player relationship: offers fame, vehicles, and high-risk events; rivals become more aggressive as the player rises.

## Conflict matrix

- Authority vs Underworld: legal control versus illicit infrastructure.
- Authority vs Settlements: protection versus autonomy.
- Authority vs Circuit: controlled order versus public spectacle.
- Underworld vs Settlements: extraction versus reconstruction.
- Underworld vs Circuit: secret deals versus public prestige.
- Settlements vs Circuit: community safety versus reckless fame.

## Reputation

Each faction uses -100 to +100 with thresholds at -60, -20, +20, and +60. Every reputation change must carry an event identifier and reason that can be shown in the UI.
