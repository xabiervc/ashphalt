# Campaign stage specification

## Stage structure

Each city stage contains:

1. Arrival and narrative context.
2. Workshop/garage preparation.
3. One required qualifying event.
4. Two or more optional events.
5. One rival or faction confrontation.
6. A city Chaos Rating escalation.
7. A stage decision.
8. Departure and route selection.

## Minimum stage content

- 1 signature race.
- 1 destruction event.
- 1 pursuit or escape event.
- 1 protection, delivery, or sabotage event.
- 1 named rival appearance.
- 1 faction-specific consequence.
- 1 weather/world-event modifier.
- 1 workshop or salvage opportunity.

## Stage completion

The player may qualify through speed, destruction, protection, or a negotiated faction path. A stage's score includes placement, Chaos Rating, optional objectives, vehicle condition, reputation, and collateral consequences.

## Quality bar

Every stage needs a distinctive memory, a readable local conflict, at least two viable strategies, and a fail-forward state. It must not feel like the previous city with different textures.
