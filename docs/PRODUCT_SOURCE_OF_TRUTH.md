# Ashphalt product source of truth

## Authority order

1. `docs/PRODUCT_SOURCE_OF_TRUTH.md` — scope, canon, mandatory/aspirational status, and conflict resolution.
2. `docs/CORE_GAMEPLAY_SPECIFICATION.md` — player experience and system loop.
3. `docs/OPEN_WORLD_VERTICAL_SLICE.md` and `docs/VERTICAL_SLICE_READINESS_GATE.md` — first playable proof and gate.
4. Specialist domain documents — implementation details.
5. Archived or superseded documents — historical context only; never implementation authority.

## Product identity

Ashphalt: Aftermath is a single-player open-city arcade vehicular-combat racing game. The player enters The Ash Race, explores five fictional cities, turns speed and destruction into score and leverage, builds vehicles with meaningful trade-offs, and changes cities/factions/rivals through persistent choices.

## Mandatory scope

- Windows PC first target; handheld evaluation later.
- One open Crown subdistrict for the first playable.
- One player vehicle, one rival, one Authority interceptor, traffic placeholders, workshop, damage, one race, one shortcut, one free-roam activity, one faction decision, one city-state consequence.
- Five open cities, approximately 25 subdistricts, and campaign content targets only after the Crown gate passes.
- Single-player only. Multiplayer is deferred.
- Fictional locations, vehicles, brands, and characters.

## Aspirational scope

- 25-35 hour campaign.
- Five fully polished cities.
- 50-75 structured events and 20-30 free-roam activities.
- 50+ hour mastery/completion target.
- Award-caliber presentation.

Aspirational scope cannot block the first playable or be used to justify adding content before evidence.

## Conflict rule

When documents conflict, stop implementation and open a decision entry. Do not silently choose the newest filename or the most ambitious option.

## Change rule

Every approved product change must update this document, the relevant specialist document, traceability, roadmap, and progress status in the same commit.
