# Product source of truth

## Product canon

`GDD.md` is the product brief and fixed canon. It defines Ashphalt: Aftermath, The Ash Race, The Fire Key, The Gate, Chaos Rating, five open cities, persistent city states, and single-player-first scope.

## Implementation authority

Use `CANONICAL_PREPRODUCTION_INDEX.md` for document hierarchy, `PRODUCTION_SCOPE_FREEZE.md` for mandatory scope, and `REQUIREMENT_OBLIGATION_MATRIX.md` for requirement obligations.

## Evidence authority

Use `QUALITY_EVIDENCE_REGISTER.md`, QA documents, prototype reports, playtest records, and signed gates. Documentation alone cannot mark implementation or quality as validated.
