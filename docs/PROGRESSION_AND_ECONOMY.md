# Progression and economy

## Resources

- Credits: repairs, tuning, entry fees, bribes.
- Parts: upgrades and salvage.
- Reputation: faction access and consequences.
- Vehicles: unlockable platforms, recovered rivals, and faction rewards.
- Information: route and event intelligence.

## Progression

Progression is horizontal first: access to different tools, routes, factions, and event types. Vertical upgrades must be balanced with costs and weaknesses.

## Workshop economy

Prices depend on component damage, installed upgrades, faction standing, scarcity, and event urgency. The player must always have a viable recovery path after failure.

## Anti-grind rules

Provide multiple ways to earn resources. Avoid requiring repeated identical races. Give meaningful rewards for optional objectives and exploration. Use a soft catch-up mechanism if the player falls behind.

## Balance tests

Simulate a conservative, aggressive, unlucky, and opportunistic player profile. Each should reach the next meaningful decision without an economic dead end.
