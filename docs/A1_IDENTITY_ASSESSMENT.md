# A1 — Identity assessment

## Closed decisions

- Genre and player-facing fantasy are defined in the canonical GDD and vision documents.
- Target player, experience promise, pillars, anti-pillars, scope boundary, session format, and intended differentiation are stated.
- Out-of-scope features are recorded to prevent scope drift.

## Reviewer test

A reviewer can answer what Ashphalt is, who it serves, what makes it distinct, and what it refuses to become without inventing a design decision.

## Not yet knowable

Market performance and emotional resonance require playtesting.
