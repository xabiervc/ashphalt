# Challenge and difficulty curve

## Difficulty axes

- Driving precision.
- Traffic density.
- Rival aggression.
- Authority escalation.
- Weather severity.
- Route complexity.
- Resource pressure.
- Consequence permanence.

Increase one or two axes at a time. Do not spike all axes simultaneously without a clear authored set piece.

## Campaign curve

- Prologue: teach driving, damage, workshop, one choice.
- The Crown: route reading and basic pursuit.
- The Works: destruction and heavy vehicles.
- The Verge: protection and trade-offs.
- The Salt Run: speed, navigation, weather.
- The Lowlands: changing routes, emergencies, persistence.
- The Gate: combined mastery and narrative payoff.

## Recoverability target

At least 70% of ordinary failures should allow recovery or a meaningful fail-forward state. Catastrophic loss must be telegraphed and generally require accumulated risk or an explicit high-stakes choice.
