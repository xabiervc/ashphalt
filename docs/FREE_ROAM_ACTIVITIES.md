# Free-roam activities

## Activity types

- Spontaneous rival challenge.
- Police heat escape.
- Salvage cache recovery.
- Workshop delivery.
- Settlement rescue.
- Convoy encounter.
- Destruction hotspot.
- Shortcut discovery.
- Emergency response.
- Time-trial beacon.
- Vehicle recovery.
- Faction patrol disruption.

## Activity rules

Activities have an ID, spawn conditions, district, seed, participants, objective, reward, reputation effects, expiration, repeat policy, and aftermath. Repeated activities use modifiers instead of identical repetition.

## Target volume

The full campaign targets 20-30 meaningful free-roam activities across the five cities. The vertical slice requires at least one repeatable and one consequence-bearing activity.
