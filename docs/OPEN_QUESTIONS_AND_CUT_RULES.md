# Open questions and cut rules

## Production decisions

- Exact Unreal Engine version.
- Final PC minimum/recommended hardware.
- Target resolution/FPS profile.
- Final title trademark clearance.
- Content rating and pedestrian presentation.
- Audio/voice production budget.
- Team capacity and milestone dates.

## Cut rules

Cut or defer a feature when it fails to support the player fantasy, duplicates an existing interaction, exceeds the performance budget, cannot be explained in the first hour, creates unacceptable accessibility barriers, or lacks a testable acceptance criterion.

## Decision rule

Open questions are not hidden assumptions. Each must have an owner, decision deadline, affected documents, fallback option, and cost of delay.
