# Open risks and assumptions

## Risks

- Chaos Vehicle feel may require custom handling beyond default settings.
- Open-city streaming and destruction may exceed target hardware budgets.
- High NPC density can reduce readability or performance.
- Pedestrian presentation may affect rating and player comfort.
- Five-city scope may exceed a small-team schedule.
- The title requires professional trademark clearance.
- Music/voice production may exceed the available budget.

## Assumptions to validate

- Players enjoy leaving race routes.
- Chaos Rating creates meaningful strategy rather than score farming.
- City-state persistence is noticed and valued.
- Upgrade trade-offs change decisions.
- 25-35 hours is an appropriate campaign target.
- The Crown can stream continuously at target performance.

Each risk/assumption needs an owner, experiment, success threshold, and decision date.
