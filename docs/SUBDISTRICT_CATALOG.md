# Subdistrict catalog

## The Crown

- Financial Ruins: dense towers, broken avenues, vertical sightlines.
- Old Transit: tunnels, stations, maintenance shortcuts.
- Civic Spire: Authority controls and checkpoint events.
- Flooded Underground: water hazards and emergency routes.
- Authority Ring: high-heat pursuit and elite response.

## The Works

- Container Docks: cranes, cargo lanes, heavy vehicles.
- Refinery Belt: fire hazards, pipes, toxic runoff.
- Foundry Row: narrow industrial corridors and destruction events.
- Crane Yard: vertical machinery and route hazards.
- Black Market Mile: workshops, brokers, and ambushes.

## The Verge

- Settlement Core: safehouse, workshops, social consequences.
- Abandoned Suburbs: alleys, homes, hidden salvage.
- Solar Fields: open routes and long sightlines.
- Broken Beltway: high-speed convoy events.
- Orchard Road: protection, evacuation, and weather routes.

## The Salt Run

- Salt Flats: maximum-speed racing.
- Wind Farm: crosswinds and infrastructure obstacles.
- Dry River: off-road shortcuts.
- Relay Highway: long-distance pursuit.
- Storm Basin: sandstorm escalation and shelter routes.

## The Lowlands

- Flooded Center: water and rerouting.
- Levee Road: defensive convoys and Authority access.
- Sunken Mall: enclosed destruction arena.
- Emergency Quarter: rescue and protected-unit events.
- Collapsed Causeway: changing bridge routes and finale preparation.
