# Voice and radio specification

## Voice pillars

Short, reactive, character-specific, and useful. Radio should make the city feel alive and communicate objectives, threats, and consequences without becoming constant chatter.

## Voice content target

- Core cast: 8 named voices or stylized text/audio equivalents.
- Rival barks: race, damage, defeat, rescue, betrayal, city-state, and comeback contexts.
- Authority: escalation, warning, pursuit, roadblock, disengagement.
- Settlement/emergency: convoy, rescue, distress, aftermath.
- Radio/news: city state, race rumors, Fire Key clues, faction reactions.

## Data

Every line has ID, speaker, context, priority, interruptibility, localization key, subtitle, caption metadata, fallback, and mix ducking class.

## Accessibility

All essential voice information has text/subtitle and non-audio visual support. Radio can be reduced or disabled without removing objectives.
