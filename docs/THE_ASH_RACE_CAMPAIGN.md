# The Ash Race campaign

## Canon

- Game: **Ashphalt: Aftermath**.
- Competition: **The Ash Race**.
- Core artifact: **The Fire Key**.
- Final destination: **The Gate**.
- Destruction meter: **Chaos Rating**.
- Maximum city state: **Ash State**.

## Premise

The Ash Race is a clandestine, continent-spanning competition through isolated post-collapse cities. Its drivers are attracted to speed, spectacle, money, revenge, fame, and destruction. The public sees an outlaw sport. The factions see a moving political weapon.

The winner is promised access to The Fire Key, an old-world authorization core that can restart the network controlling the last functioning energy and communications corridors. The Key is not magic and is not simply a trophy: it is a technology whose control could reconnect cities, empower one faction, or create a new monopoly.

## Player role

The player is a rising driver entering the race with a damaged vehicle, an obligation to their home settlement, and a personal reason to discover who created the route. The player can pursue victory, chaos, protection, profit, revenge, or a changing mixture of motives.

## Campaign acts

### Prologue — Starting Line

The player repairs an incomplete vehicle and receives a message connecting a missing contact to The Fire Key. The first event introduces racing, destruction, workshop recovery, Chaos Rating, and the first faction decision.

### Act I — The Crown

Dense urban routes, Authority checkpoints, first rivalries, and the discovery that race checkpoints are being manipulated.

### Act II — The Works

Industrial districts, Underworld workshops, illegal upgrades, sabotage, and evidence that competitors are being steered toward specific targets.

### Act III — The Verge

Settlement routes, convoy protection, civilian consequences, and the decision whether to prioritize the race or preserve a community's infrastructure.

### Act IV — The Salt Run

Long-distance stages, sandstorms, ambushes, alliances, and the revelation that The Circuit does not control the whole race.

### Act V — The Lowlands

Flooded routes, emergency response, collapsing infrastructure, and the discovery that The Fire Key can either reconnect or control the cities.

### Finale — The Gate

The surviving drivers reach the installation. The outcome depends on Chaos Rating, faction standing, rescues, sabotage, alliances, and the player's major decisions.

## Core outcomes

- Deliver The Fire Key to The Authority.
- Release the network to the cities through a coalition.
- Sell control to The Underworld.
- Give The Circuit ownership of the spectacle and routes.
- Destroy The Fire Key to prevent a new monopoly.
- Lose control of The Gate after creating too much chaos.

No ending is labelled simply good or evil. Each expresses a different answer to the question: who should control movement, energy, and information after collapse?

## Quality requirements

Each act needs a memorable set piece, a distinctive district identity, a rival relationship change, a mechanically visible consequence, and a failure/recovery path. Story content must pass the narrative-quality and vertical-slice gates before expansion.
