# City size and density

## Targets

- Five major open cities.
- Approximately five subdistricts per city.
- Approximately 25 subdistricts total.
- 10-15 minutes to cross a city using the main route.
- 20-30 minutes to explore without an objective.
- 2-4 hours of initial activities per city.
- 4-6 hours for deep completion per city.
- 25-35 hours for the campaign target, subject to playtest validation.

## Density metrics

Each subdistrict must contain a landmark, primary route, alternate route, workshop/safe point, structured event, free-roam activity, faction presence, emergency space, and a recoverable route if the player gets lost.

## Anti-bloat rule

Do not increase map size unless the additional space adds a new interaction, route decision, event, landmark, or narrative consequence. Measure content per minute of driving, not only area.
