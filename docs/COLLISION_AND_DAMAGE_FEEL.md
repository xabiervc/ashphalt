# Collision and damage feel

## Collision categories

- Glancing traffic hit: loses line, low component damage.
- Controlled side bash: transfers momentum, medium body damage.
- Frontal ram: high impulse, mutual risk, attachment interaction.
- Wall scrape: sustained low damage and speed loss.
- Major impact: component damage, debris, camera/audio response.
- Catastrophic impact: rare, telegraphed, usually after accumulated damage.

## Readability sequence

Impact anticipation → contact sound/feedback → physical response → component result → HUD/status → strategic consequence.

## Balance target

The player should frequently survive mistakes but never ignore damage. A successful aggressive driver finishes damaged, adapts route and handling, repairs, and remembers the risk.

## Test metrics

Record impact speed, angle, mass ratio, damage applied, recovery time, player understanding, and whether the outcome felt deserved.
