# Free-roam objective contract

## Activity: The Stray Signal

A damaged racer broadcasts a short-range distress signal from Old Transit. The player can follow the signal, find the vehicle, and choose to repair, tow, raid, or ignore it.

## States

- Hidden.
- Signal detected.
- Approaching.
- Contact.
- Resolved.
- Cooldown.

## Outcomes

- Repair: Settlement reputation, delayed rival assistance.
- Tow: credits and parts, vehicle workshop availability.
- Raid: Underworld reputation, parts, Authority heat.
- Ignore: no immediate reward, rival may appear later in a different state.

## Requirements

The activity must be discoverable without blocking the main race, show a readable risk/reward preview, persist its resolution, and be testable under all four outcome paths.
