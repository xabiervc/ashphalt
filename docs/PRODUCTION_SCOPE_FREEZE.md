# Production scope freeze

## Frozen mandatory prototype scope

- Unreal project foundation.
- Track A vehicle-control prototype.
- Track B open Crown challenge prototype.
- Track C progression prototype.
- The Crown five-zone blockout.
- One vehicle, one rival, one Authority interceptor.
- One circuit event, one destruction event, one free-roam activity.
- Workshop and three upgrades.
- Component damage and recovery.
- One weather route change.
- One faction choice and city-state consequence.
- Baseline HUD, accessibility, save/load, debug commands, and profiling.

## Objective for vertical slice

Polished Crown presentation, three events, four characters, audio/VFX/animation placeholders, aftermath scene, replay fixture, and external playtest evidence.

## Aspirational/deferred

Five complete cities, full campaign content, final voice/music production, maximum crowd density, local multiplayer, online multiplayer, matchmaking, live service, and platform certification.

## Change control

Any feature added to the frozen prototype must identify what is removed, deferred, or given additional budget. “Just one more feature” is not an approved scope change.
