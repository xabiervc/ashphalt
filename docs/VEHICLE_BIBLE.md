# Vehicle bible

See `VEHICLE_CATALOG.md` for the initial quantified roster. Every vehicle uses fictional manufacturers and models, separate component health, tuning slots, repair economics, faction tags, and AI parity.

The starting roster contains a heavy brawler, agile runner, off-road hauler, fast fragile vehicle, and utility van. Police and faction vehicles are defined separately in `POLICE_AND_EMERGENCY_CATALOG.md` and future faction data assets.
