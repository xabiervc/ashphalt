# Quality promotion criteria

## Levels

- **Documented A:** complete canonical design and controlled prototype scope.
- **Validated A:** Q1-Q3 plus accessibility, performance, persistence, narrative, and repeatability evidence passed.
- **Production approved:** validated A plus owned risks, budget/schedule confidence, content pipeline, legal/rating decisions, and signed production plan.

## Non-negotiable

No percentage claim can replace evidence. A failed gate is useful data and requires a design or implementation decision, not relabelling.
