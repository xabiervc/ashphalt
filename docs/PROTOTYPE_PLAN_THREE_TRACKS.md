# Three-track prototype plan

## Track A — Control prototype

Question: is driving precise and satisfying?

Contents: one vehicle, benchmark track, camera, controls, braking, steering, handbrake, recovery, impact wall, weather strip.

Pass: Q1 handling metrics in `VEHICLE_FEEL_METRICS.md`, blind testers understand control, no critical physics instability.

## Track B — Challenge prototype

Question: can players read routes, threats, and failure causes?

Contents: open Crown blockout, official route, shortcut, landmark, rival, Authority interceptor, one weather route change, objective HUD.

Pass: players choose routes, identify threats, understand failures, and recover without coaching.

## Track C — Progression prototype

Question: do upgrades change decisions?

Contents: three upgrades, workshop, two route variants, one rival configuration, one faction consequence, before/after comparison.

Pass: players choose different upgrades for different objectives and can explain the trade-off.

## Sequencing

Build A first. Build B only after A is controllable. Build C only after B is readable. Do not combine all unknowns in the first test.
