# Campaign content budget

## Production target

| Content | Per city | Total target |
|---|---:|---:|
| Subdistricts | 5 | 25 |
| Main events | 1 | 5 |
| Secondary races | 4-6 | 20-30 |
| Destruction events | 2-3 | 10-15 |
| Pursuit/escape events | 1-2 | 5-10 |
| Convoy/delivery/rescue events | 2-3 | 10-15 |
| Free-roam activities | 4-6 | 20-30 |
| Named rival appearances | 2-4 | 10-20 |

## Duration target

Campaign completion target is 25-35 hours, with additional time for mastery, alternate choices, vehicle collection, and high Chaos Rating runs. These numbers remain hypotheses until playtested.

## Quality over volume

Every event must add a new route decision, mechanic combination, narrative consequence, rival interaction, or mastery challenge. Repetition without a meaningful modifier is not acceptable.
