# Narrative consequence matrix

The most important persistent consequence is the city state. Player actions can leave a city Stable, Disturbed, Violent, Burning, Collapsing, or in Ash State. This affects future routes, traffic, Authority response, economy, emergency activity, faction control, rival relationships, and ending influence.

See `CITY_STATE_MACHINE.md` and `CAMPAIGN_QA_MATRIX.md` for transition rules and tests.
