# Narrative consequence matrix

| Player behavior | Immediate effect | Later effect | Possible ending influence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Maximize Chaos Rating | score, fame, Authority heat | stronger patrols, frightened settlements | collapse or Circuit dominance |
| Protect convoys | lower immediate score | settlement routes and allies | coalition/reconstruction |
| Destroy Authority units | high score, heat | elite response and checkpoints | Underworld or freedom path |
| Accept illegal upgrades | vehicle advantage | scans, debt, Underworld leverage | Underworld control |
| Help a rival | lose short-term advantage | alliance, rescue, alternate ending support | coalition or Circuit alliance |
| Sabotage a rival | easier event | persistent vendetta and ambushes | personal victory, unstable aftermath |
| Keep a city below Ash State | lower spectacle rewards | infrastructure remains usable | reconstruction outcomes |
| Push a city into Ash State | maximum spectacle | blocked routes, displaced factions | collapse/monopoly outcomes |
| Rescue emergency workers | lower destruction opportunity | emergency support and safe routes | coalition/Authority outcomes |
| Destroy protected emergency assets | high risk score | faction hostility and narrative lock | destructive endings |

Every row must be represented by event IDs, reputation changes, save data, UI feedback, and at least one test scenario.
