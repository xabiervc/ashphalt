# UX and presentation specification

## Clarity hierarchy

At any moment the player should be able to identify current objective, vehicle condition, immediate threat, available action, and recent consequence within seconds.

## Feedback language

Every major event combines at least two channels from visual, audio, haptic, text, or camera response. Critical information uses three where appropriate and remains available in a log or HUD state.

## Presentation identity

Ashphalt uses damaged urban geometry, aggressive silhouettes, readable color/value grouping, industrial sound design, expressive vehicle damage, and a controlled contrast between grim infrastructure and absurd arcade spectacle.

## Camera and impact

Camera shake and impact effects are proportional, adjustable, and never obscure control. The player must understand whether an impact helped, harmed, or merely looked dramatic.

## UX testing

Measure time to find a workshop, time to understand a faction change, mistaken menu selections, unreadable HUD states, and whether players can recover after losing a race.
